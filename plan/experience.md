# The Experience

You walk into a darkened room, filled with sparse glowing rectangles and chirps and squelches. You scan a QR code near the entrance and your own rectangle begins to glow. You tap the screen and a periodic mewling sound joins the din of the room.

At first, it feels chaotic and disorganized: many different sounds, timings, and tunings, a bit like an orchestra before the performance begins. But then they all start shifting. The pitches change, the timings start to come together, until suddenly they are working in unison, pulsing a giant, steady chord, chiming and flashing together.

A wobbly question mark appears on your screen, and then two organic colored shapes appear below it. After a moment of hesitation, the word "choose" appears beneath them. You tap the green triangle. Other participants around you are making similar choices. And then, suddenly, the room is divided into two groups. The beat starts to separate into two components. A call and response between the two groups starts to form.

Another choice appears on screens, and then the group is divided into four. The music organizes itself into a four-part composition, each subgroup acting together. Several more choices appear on screens and transform the auditory and visual environment in various ways.

And then they all come back together again. Sounds sweep across the room from one side to the other. The phones seem to know where they are in the room. The piece comes to a climax, and the lights come on.
